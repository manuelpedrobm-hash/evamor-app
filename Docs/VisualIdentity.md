## Encargo usado para generar la versión 2

Modo: herramienta integrada de generación de imagen, creación desde texto.

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: square master artwork for an original iPhone meditation app, usable both as the completion illustration and as the source for the app icon. Primary request: create a distinctive, calm abstract symbol of stillness and benevolence: a small warm golden center held by three softly irregular concentric stone-like rings, with two very subtle upward organic arcs suggesting breath and growth without resembling a lotus, religious emblem, or existing meditation brand. Scene/backdrop: full-bleed deep forest-green square background. Style/medium: refined minimal editorial illustration, tactile cut-paper and mineral pigment texture, crisp silhouette at small icon sizes, premium native-iOS feeling. Composition/framing: perfectly centered, generous breathing room, strong simple geometry, no border. Lighting/mood: quiet, grounded, compassionate, contemplative; very subtle depth and soft natural shading. Color palette: deep forest green, warm limestone, muted sage, restrained ochre-gold center. Constraints: entirely original; readable at 64 px; no text, no letters, no watermark, no people, no faces, no hands, no Buddha, no lotus, no wheel, no mandala, no temple, no institutional or religious symbols, no gradients that turn muddy at icon size. Avoid: glossy 3D, neon, photorealism, clip-art flower, generic wellness stock art, copied app branding.

Antes de publicación conviene comprobar el icono en un iPhone real, especialmente a tamaños pequeños y con los tratamientos visuales que aplique la versión de iOS usada para distribuir.
